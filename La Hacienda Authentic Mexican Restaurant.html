Cooked like home

La Hacienda has served Springfield’s south side on Glenstone Avenue with traditional Mexican recipes made from scratch. Sauces simmer all day, tortillas are warm, and every plate comes out the way a family would serve it at their own table.

Stop in after work, bring the kids on the weekend, or book a big table for a birthday. You will leave full.
